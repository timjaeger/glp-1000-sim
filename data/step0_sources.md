## Adoption and population

- **S1** Gallup, "In U.S., GLP-1 Usage Reaches New High" (Jul 7, 2026; updated Sep 8, 2026). 11% of US adults currently use a GLP-1 for weight loss, 15% ever; 3%/6% in 2024; survey of 5,065 adults, May 28–Jun 5, 2026. Obesity 39.9% (2022) → 36.4% (2026). https://news.gallup.com/poll/712157/glp-usage-reaches-new-high.aspx
- **S2** Medscape summary of the same (Jul 10, 2026): awareness 80%→91%. https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/1-9-americans-now-glp-1s-2026a1000nds
- **S3** KFF Health Tracking Poll (Oct 27–Nov 2, 2025, n=1,350), via Lindy Health and GLP Winner aggregations: 12% current (all reasons), 18% ever; ages 50–64 ≈22%, 65+ ≈9%; 57% of adults with diabetes and 40% with heart disease have used one. (secondary) https://lindyhealth.com/statistics/glp-1-statistics/ ; https://www.glpwinner.com/insights/whos-using-glp-1s-now-the-2026-numbers-explained
- **S4** Orandi et al., *Pediatrics* (Sep 4, 2026), "Trends in GLP-1 receptor agonist prescriptions for children ages 8 to 11 with obesity: 2019–2026" — 0.03%→9.3% among prescription events; 0.6% of 3.5M children with obesity overall; 20,282 children; upper-income +55%. Via AJMC (Sep 10, 2026). https://www.ajmc.com/view/glp-1-prescribing-in-young-children-with-obesity-rises-310-fold
- **S5** Rodriguez et al., *JAMA Network Open* (Jan 2025, n=125,474): most weight-management starters discontinue within a year; 36–47% of stoppers restart within a year. Via Levels. (secondary) https://www.levels.com/pro/enterprise/glp-1-discontinuation
- **S6** AJMC (Apr 14, 2026): persistence 65% at 120 days → 34% at one year; OOP ≥$150 doubles discontinuation. https://www.pulse.bot/healthcare/news/gaps-in-persistence-coverage-limit-glp-1-impact-in-obesity-3cb14296-042e-46e9-9e5e-2b66c9ff4cf7/
- **S7** Pabau GLP-1 statistics roundup (2026): prescribing per 100k adults 1,884 (Q2 2021) → 8,819 (Q1 2026); 37% know someone taking one. (secondary) https://pabau.com/blog/glp-1-statistics/
